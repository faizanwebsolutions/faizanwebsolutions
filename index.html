Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

Everything you need to know about working with Faizan Web Solutions before starting your project.

How long does it take to design and launch my website?

Typically, standard business websites take 7–14 business days from kickoff to final launch. Custom WordPress web applications or e-commerce solutions take around 2–4 weeks depending on features and custom design complexity.

Will I be able to update content on my site without coding?

Yes! Whether we build you a custom ACF-powered WordPress theme or a streamlined Elementor site, you get a clean, intuitive dashboard. Edit text, swap images, update prices, and post new services in just a few clicks — no coding, no broken layouts.

Is technical SEO and speed optimization included?

Absolute necessity! Every single site we deliver is optimized for sub-second loading speeds (Google PageSpeed 90+ score), proper HTML heading hierarchy, schema markup, and responsive mobile-first SEO indexing.

How do payment terms work for international/local clients?

We usually work on a 50% deposit upfront to initiate research and architecture design, and 50% upon project completion and your 100% sign-off before server deployment.

Do you offer post-launch maintenance and support?

Yes! All packages include 30 days of free post-launch support to ensure everything operates smoothly. We also offer ongoing monthly maintenance packages for updates, security backups, and continuous optimizations.
