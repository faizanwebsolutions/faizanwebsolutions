Our Story

Why We Built Faizan Web Solutions

Faizan Web Solutions was born from a frustration every business owner knows: paying for a beautiful website that loads slowly and converts nothing. Too many agencies hide bloated, plugin-stuffed templates behind pretty mockups — then hand over a site that fails Core Web Vitals, bleeds mobile traffic, and quietly costs you customers every single day.

We decided to do the opposite. Every build starts from a lean, high-performance foundation — a custom-coded WordPress theme or an optimized page-builder build, engineered around your conversion goals, not a theme demo's. The result is an enterprise web architecture mindset applied to businesses of every size: sub-second load times, technical SEO baked in from day one, and a site your team can actually manage.

We replace bloated templates with lean, high-performance builds — because your website should be a revenue engine, not a digital brochure.
